Project Methodology & Technical Architecture

Comprehensive overview of data processing, analytical modeling, and system integration.

1. Data Ingestion & Hygiene

The analysis is constructed from 101 authentic student survey responses. The raw data underwent systematic preprocessing in Python, including imputation of missing demographic metrics using median values, standardizing mixed-casing entries, and parsing academic performance bands into quantitative indicators.

2. Analytical Metrics & Calculated Fields

  • Composite Risk Category: Multi-condition logic evaluating whether students exhibit single or co-occurring symptoms of depression, anxiety, and panic attacks.
  • Treatment Deficit Index: Quantifies students facing multi-symptom distress who have not engaged with medical or campus psychological counseling services.
  • Faculty Stratification: Course majors mapped into 6 macro-faculties (Engineering, IT/CS, Law, Business, Humanities, and Health Sciences) for macro-level comparison.

3. Web Delivery & Embedding Pipeline

Visualizations published on Tableau Public are integrated into this responsive Flask application via the Tableau JavaScript API. Dynamic script sizing guarantees optimal readability across desktop, tablet, and mobile devices.
