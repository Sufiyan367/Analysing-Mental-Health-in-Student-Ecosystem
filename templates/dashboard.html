Dashboard Layout & Panel Specification

The dashboard integrates 4 verified KPI cards and 6 specialized analytical panels engineered strictly from the 18 dataset columns:

Panel 1: Stress Level Distribution

Vertical bar chart presenting exact counts and percentages across Low (22.0%), Medium (53.5%), and High (24.5%) cohorts.

Panel 2: Symptom Escalation

Dual-measure grouped bar chart showing steep anxiety (72.06) and depression (68.78) surges in high-stress students.

Panel 3: Sleep Quality vs Stress

Segmented stacked bar chart revealing that 71.4% of high-stress individuals experience poor sleep.

Panel 4: Screen Time Exposure

Continuous measure bar chart displaying 8.12 hrs/day screen immersion for distressed students (+2.12 hrs above low-stress).

Panel 5: Prior History Distribution

Donut chart illustrating that 60% of students bring no prior mental-health diagnoses into university studies.

Panel 6: Ranked Therapy Efficacy

Horizontal ranked bar chart showing Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT) leading clinical improvement with a 40.80 progress score.
