Key Analytical Findings

  • The Institutional Care Gap: While 27.7% of surveyed students fall into the High Risk tier (co-occurring depression and anxiety/panic attacks), 78.6% of these high-risk students have never sought specialist treatment.
  • Academic Vulnerability Peaks: Students in their foundational and penultimate transition years (Year 1 and Year 3) display heightened susceptibility to acute panic events and depressive symptoms.
  • Academic Performance Disconnect: High-performing students (CGPA 3.50 – 4.00) experience comparable mental distress rates to students with lower CGPA brackets, indicating stress is systemic rather than purely grade-deficit driven.
  • Disciplinary Clustering: STEM disciplines (Engineering and Computer Science) exhibit dense clusters of unaddressed anxiety and stress.
