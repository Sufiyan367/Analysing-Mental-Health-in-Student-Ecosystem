Key Diagnostic Findings Across the Ecosystem

1 Symptom Escalation

Students experiencing High Stress demonstrate a >135% surge in anxiety (72.06 vs 30.27) and depression (68.78 vs 26.80) relative to their Low Stress peers, validating direct symptom coupling.

2 Compounded Sleep Deficit

Among the 49 High Stress students, 71.4% (35 students) suffer from Poor sleep, compounded by an average of 8.12 hours of daily screen exposure (+2.12 hrs over Low Stress).

3 First-Onset Distress

While 40% of students bring a prior mental-health history, 60% report no prior diagnoses. This group contains 66 Medium-stress and 14 High-stress individuals undergoing first-onset university distress.

4 Intervention Efficacy

Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT) yields the highest clinical recovery with an average progress score of 40.80 across 7.50 weeks, followed by Counseling (34.43) and Support Groups (33.10).
