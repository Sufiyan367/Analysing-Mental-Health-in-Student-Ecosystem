5-Scene Analytical Narrative Architecture

  1. Scene 1 — Cohort Baseline: Establishes the overall demographic makeup and initial benchmark prevalence of mental health indicators across the student population.
  2. Scene 2 — The Academic Pressure Paradigm: Demonstrates that high CGPA performance does not insulate students from severe anxiety or depressive symptoms.
  3. Scene 3 — Lifestyle Stress Vectors: Evaluates study workload intensity, sleep disturbances, and acute panic attack occurrences.
  4. Scene 4 — Vulnerability Mapping: Isolates the 27.7% High-Risk cohort and contrasts symptom burden against the alarming 78.6% treatment deficit.
  5. Scene 5 — Strategic Roadmap: Prescribes concrete institutional solutions: proactive mental wellness screenings, de-stigmatized peer support, and academic calendar pacing.
