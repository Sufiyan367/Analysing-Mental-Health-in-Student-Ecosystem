📖 Guided 5-Scene Data Story

A structured analytical narrative taking institutional stakeholders from population baseline spread through symptom escalation, behavioral risks, and clinical recovery.

Scene 01

Student Mental Health Baseline

Establishes the macro-level distribution of stress across the 200-student university ecosystem. Over 78% of students experience elevated chronic distress.

  • Low Stress: 44 students (22.0% of cohort)
  • Medium Stress: 107 students (53.5% of cohort)
  • High Stress: 49 students (24.5% of cohort)
Scene 02

Stress & Psychological Symptoms

Quantifies how subjective perceived stress translates into standardized clinical anxiety and depression loads, revealing dramatic symptom surges.

  • Anxiety Escalation: Surges from 30.27 (Low) to 72.06 (High) (+138%)
  • Depression Escalation: Surges from 26.80 (Low) to 68.78 (High) (+156%)
  • Gender Parity: Both male and female students experience balanced severity burdens
Scene 03

Lifestyle Factors & Stress Compounding

Isolates the lifestyle triad—sleep deprivation, screen immersion, and physical inactivity—that compounds psychological deterioration.

  • Severe Sleep Deficit: 71.4% (35 of 49) of high-stress students report Poor sleep
  • Screen Immersion: High-stress students average 8.12 hours/day on devices
  • Physical Inactivity: 75.5% of the high-stress cohort engages in low physical exercise
Scene 04

Vulnerability & Support Systems

Examines pre-existing clinical history against first-onset university distress and highlights the protective buffer of peer support networks.

  • Pre-Existing History: 40.0% (80 students) have a diagnosed clinical history
  • First-Onset Distress: 60.0% had no prior history, yet 14 high-stress cases emerged
  • Support Network Buffer: Strong peer systems correlate with low stress concentrations
Scene 05

Intervention Patterns & Clinical Recovery

Ranks psychological therapy modalities by empirical progress scores and exposes the critical institutional care deficit.

  • Top Recovery Efficacy: CBT achieves highest progress score (40.80 / 100)
  • Broad Benefits: Counseling (34.43), Support Groups (33.10), Meditation (32.57)
  • Care Gap: 53.5% (107 students) receive No Therapy on campus

🔍 Key Empirical Discoveries

Four evidence-based takeaways extracted from the validated student mental health ecosystem dataset.

🚨

1. Pervasive Stress Burden

78.0% of the student cohort operates in moderate or severe stress tiers, showing that psychological strain is an ecosystem-wide challenge rather than an isolated outlier.

📈

2. Symptom Escalation

High-stress students experience a >135% surge in anxiety (72.06) and depression (68.78) relative to low-stress peers, proving a direct coupling between subjective stress and clinical symptom load.

📱

3. Behavioral Compounding

71.4% of high-stress students suffer from severe sleep deficits paired with 8.12 hours of screen immersion, confirming that digital saturation exacerbates emotional exhaustion.

🩹

4. Institutional Care Gap

53.5% of students receive No Therapy, including 15 high-stress and 48 medium-stress students. Structured Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT) achieves top recovery (40.80 score).
