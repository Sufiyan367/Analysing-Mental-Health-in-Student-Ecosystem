# Project Overview & Problem Statement

## Project Title
**Analysing Mental Health in Student Ecosystem**

## Course / Program
**Data Analytics with Tableau**  
*Virtual Internship Program — SmartBridge / SkillWallet*

---

## 1. Problem Statement
The modern higher education ecosystem places severe psychological, emotional, and cognitive demands on university students. Compounding pressures including intensive academic workloads, lifestyle dysregulation, altered sleep patterns, and prolonged digital screen immersion significantly contribute to escalating mental health distress—specifically elevated anxiety, depression, and chronic stress.

Despite the widespread prevalence of these challenges, mental health support across academic ecosystems often remains reactive rather than predictive. Educational institutions frequently lack centralized, empirical diagnostic frameworks to:
1. Identify high-risk student cohorts early in their academic trajectories.
2. Measure the correlational impact of sleep deprivation and digital screen time on mental well-being.
3. Understand symptom escalation across self-reported stress tiers.
4. Assess the care deficit between psychological distress and active therapeutic support.

---

## 2. Project Objectives
* **Data Ingestion & Integrity Validation:** Ingest, audit, and certify a standardized 200-student, 18-variable psychometric dataset (`mental_health_student_ecosystem_cleaned.csv`) with zero missing values or data distortions.
* **Exploratory Visual Analysis:** Design 8 unique dataset-grounded visualizations evaluating stress levels, anxiety, depression, sleep quality, screen time, mental health history, and therapy efficacy.
* **Calculated Metrics & KPIs:** Formulate 10 production calculation fields (stress flags, severe distress indicators, screen time categories, and high-risk sleep deficit flags).
* **Multi-Device Responsive Dashboard:** Engineer responsive dashboard architectures (Desktop 1920×1080, Tablet 1024×768, and Mobile) providing campus leaders with key cohort metrics and visual diagnostic panels.
* **5-Scene Guided Data Story:** Construct a sequential data story translating clinical metrics into strategic campus policy recommendations.
* **Performance Benchmarking:** Benchmark data rendering latencies, filter evaluation speeds, calculation field performance, and visualization inventory.
* **Web Integration:** Deploy a full-stack Python Flask web portal with responsive local fallbacks and dynamic Tableau Cloud embedding via `<tableau-viz>`.
* **Demonstration & Documentation:** Produce a compliant 5–7 minute walkthrough video (MP4), verbatim narration script, and a comprehensive 19-section development report in Markdown and printable PDF format.

## 3. Target Audience / Stakeholders
* **University Administrators & Academic Deans:** Strategic resource allocation, policy reforms, and mental wellness initiatives.
* **Campus Healthcare & Counseling Centers:** Early risk intervention, targeted clinical support workshops, and capacity planning.
* **Student Welfare Bodies:** Peer-support networks and digital wellness campaigns.
* **Academic Advisors & Faculty:** Workload balancing and empathetic academic mentoring.
