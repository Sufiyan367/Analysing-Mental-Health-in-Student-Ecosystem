# Project Overview & Problem Statement

## Project Title
**Analysing Mental Health in Student Ecosystem**

## Course / Program
**Data Analytics with Tableau**  
*Virtual Internship Program — SmartBridge / SkillWallet*

---

## 1. Problem Statement
The modern higher education ecosystem places unprecedented psychological, emotional, and cognitive demands on students. Compounding pressures including rigorous academic workloads, competitive CGPA benchmarks, social adjustment, financial distress, and altered sleep architecture significantly contribute to the onset and escalation of mental health disorders, notably depression, generalized anxiety, and acute panic conditions.

Despite the high prevalence of these challenges, mental health support across academic ecosystems often remains reactive rather than predictive. Institutions lack centralized, data-driven diagnostic frameworks to:
1. Identify high-risk student cohorts early in their academic trajectories.
2. Measure the correlational impact of sleep deprivation and academic workload on mental well-being.
3. Understand structural disparities across academic faculties, study years, and demographic groups.
4. Assess the gap between mental health symptom prevalence and professional treatment-seeking rates.

## 2. Project Objectives
* **Data Sourcing & Cleaning:** Curate and preprocess real-world student mental health survey data into an analytics-ready dimensional model.
* **Exploratory Visual Analysis:** Design granular visual worksheets uncovering distributions, risk segments, and behavioral trends.
* **Calculated Metrics & KPIs:** Formulate Composite Stress Indexes, High-Risk Flags, and Treatment Seeking metrics.
* **Interactive Dashboards:** Assemble two comprehensive analytical dashboards:
  - *Dashboard 1:* Student Demographics, Academic Profile & Lifestyle Patterns.
  - *Dashboard 2:* Psychological Health Risk Assessment & Stress Diagnostics.
* **Data Storytelling:** Construct a multi-scene Tableau Data Story communicating strategic, evidence-based recommendations to university administrators and counseling departments.
* **Web Integration:** Embed the published interactive Tableau dashboards into a responsive Python Flask web application.
* **End-to-End Delivery:** Maintain version-controlled GitHub documentation and achieve verified completion across all SkillWallet project epics.

## 3. Target Audience / Stakeholders
* **University Administrators & Deans:** Strategic resource allocation, policy reforms, and mental wellness curriculum integration.
* **Campus Healthcare & Psychological Counseling Services:** Early risk intervention, targeted support workshops, and capacity planning.
* **Student Welfare Bodies:** Peer-support networks and academic stress reduction campaigns.
* **Academic Advisors & Faculty:** Workload balancing and empathetic academic mentoring.
